About Us

Dedicated to saving lives in Uttara since 2015, the Uttara Blood Warriors is a community-driven network of altruistic donors focused on ensuring a safe and reliable blood supply. Our mission is to bridge the gap between donors and patients through collective empathy and clinical responsibility.

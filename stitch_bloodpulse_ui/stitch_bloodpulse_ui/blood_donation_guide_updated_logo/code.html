The Donation Journey

1

Registration

Sign in, show ID, and read required information. We'll verify your details to ensure accuracy.

2

Health Check

A quick mini-physical checking your temperature, pulse, blood pressure, and hemoglobin levels.

3

The Donation

Relax while you donate approximately one pint of blood. This step usually only takes 8-10 minutes.

4

Refresh & Recover

Enjoy snacks and drinks in our recovery area for 10-15 minutes before resuming your day.

cancel

Myth

"Donating blood hurts a lot and takes all day."

Fact

You'll only feel a slight pinch. The donation itself takes about 10 minutes, and the whole process under an hour.

cancel

Myth

"I can't donate if I have a tattoo or piercing."

Fact

If applied at a state-regulated facility, you can usually donate immediately. Otherwise, a 3-month wait applies.

Preparation Tips

water_drop Hydrate

Drink an extra 16 oz of water before your appointment.

restaurant Eat Well

Have a healthy, low-fat meal within two hours of donating.

checkroom Dress Comfortably

Wear clothing with sleeves that can be easily rolled up.

medical_services Aftercare

Keep the bandage on for a few hours. Avoid heavy lifting or strenuous exercise for the rest of the day.
